About Hon. Akarachi Amadi

Dedicated to Representation and Development

Hon. Akarachi Amadi is a Nigerian politician representing Mbaitoli/Ikeduru Federal Constituency of Imo State, Nigeria, in the Federal House of Representatives.

As a federal lawmaker, he participates in legislative activities, national policy discussions and the representation of his constituents.

This website serves as an information platform for constituency updates, projects, news, activities and development-related information.
