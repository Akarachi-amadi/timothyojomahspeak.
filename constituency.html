OUR CONSTITUENCY

Mbaitoli / Ikeduru Federal Constituency

A constituency made up of the people, communities and communities' aspirations of Mbaitoli and Ikeduru Local Government Areas in Imo State, Nigeria.

About our constituency

Our People, Our Communities

Mbaitoli/Ikeduru Federal Constituency is located in Imo State, South-East Nigeria. It comprises Mbaitoli and Ikeduru Local Government Areas, whose people and communities contribute to the social, cultural, economic and political life of the state.


This page provides a platform for sharing constituency information, community updates, development priorities and relevant public information concerning the people of Mbaitoli and Ikeduru.

Our two local government areas

The Constituency

LOCAL GOVERNMENT AREA 01

Mbaitoli LGA

Mbaitoli is one of the two Local Government Areas represented within the Mbaitoli/Ikeduru Federal Constituency.

The area comprises communities with their own history, traditions, institutions and development needs.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT AREA 02

Ikeduru LGA

Ikeduru is the second Local Government Area represented within the Mbaitoli/Ikeduru Federal Constituency.

Its communities contribute to the cultural, social and economic identity of Imo State.
