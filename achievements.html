Transparency

Documentation and Evidence

Reliable public information helps constituents understand what has been done and what remains to be done.

What Published Records Should Include

To make achievement updates useful and accountable, each published record should include relevant information such as:

  • Name and description of the programme, activity or milestone.
  • Location within Mbaitoli, Ikeduru or both communities.
  • Date or period of implementation.
  • Current status, where applicable.
  • Photographs or other supporting documentation.
  • Relevant official references or project information.
Our commitment to accurate information

Only information that has been checked and approved for publication should appear as an official achievement. Proposed activities should not be presented as completed projects.
