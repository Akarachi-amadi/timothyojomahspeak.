About Hon. Akarachi Amadi

Hon. Akarachi Amadi is a Nigerian politician and member of the Federal House of Representatives, representing the Mbaitoli/Ikeduru Federal Constituency of Imo State, Nigeria.

He emerged as a member of the National Assembly following the 2023 general elections, where he secured the mandate to represent the people of Mbaitoli and Ikeduru Federal Constituency.

As a federal lawmaker, Akarachi Amadi is involved in legislative activities, national policy discussions and the representation of his constituents. His responsibilities include participating in the formulation of laws, contributing to national development discussions and advocating for projects and programmes that can improve the lives of people in his constituency.

His political role places him among the representatives responsible for bringing the concerns, interests and developmental needs of the people of Mbaitoli and Ikeduru to the federal government.

Although he is publicly recognised for his political position, detailed information about his early childhood, parents, family background, educational qualifications and personal life has not been sufficiently verified in reliable public records.

Hon. Akarachi Amadi continues to serve as a political representative of Mbaitoli/Ikeduru Federal Constituency in Imo State.
